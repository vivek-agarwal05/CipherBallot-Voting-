CipherBallot — Secure Online Voting Prototype

An educational demonstrator illustrating how voter authentication, authenticated AES-256-GCM encrypted ballots, one-vote-per-voter atomic constraints, SHA-256 verifiable receipts, and Ed25519 digital signatures function together in an online election system.

1. Authenticated Ballot Encryption

Votes are encrypted using AES-256-GCM with 96-bit unique nonces. The encrypted ballot table stores no voter ID and no candidate ID column.

2. Verifiable Receipts

Voters receive a random receipt code and SHA-256 digest of their ballot ciphertext. Anyone can check that their ballot is recorded on the bulletin board without revealing their vote.

3. Duplicate Vote Prevention

Eligibility and participation are enforced atomically via database constraints on (election_id, voter_id), strictly separated from ballot payloads.

4. Ed25519 Result Signatures

Once an election closes, ballots are tallied and canonical results are digitally signed with an Ed25519 private key, allowing independent public verification.
