What Does This Verification Prove?

  • Included as Cast: This check proves that an encrypted ballot payload exists in the election database and matches the exact SHA-256 digest issued to you at voting time.
  • Tamper-Evident: If any party altered a single bit of your ciphertext, nonce, or tag in the database, the recomputed digest would mismatch.
  • Privacy Preservation: Because the receipt is stored independently from voter identity and the ballot is encrypted under AES-GCM, verifying this receipt does NOT disclose who you voted for or your student ID.
  • Educational Boundary: Receipt verification does not prevent a rogue tallying script from miscounting upon closing; end-to-end verifiability with mix-nets or homomorphic encryption is required for full cryptographic tally verification.
