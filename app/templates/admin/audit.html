Cryptographic Audit Architecture & Threat Model

Hash Chain Construction: Every event's digest is computed as SHA-256(prev_hash || canonical_json(safe_payload)). Modifying, deleting, or reordering any past row invalidates every subsequent hash, making retroactive tampering detectable during Verify Audit Chain.

Limitation: An attacker with full database root access who replaces the entire database and recomputes all hashes from genesis could rewrite history unless external periodic checkpoints or an independent external witness (such as a public transparency log or RFC 6962 tree) are anchored outside the server.
